QUISARC

Quantifying the Impact of South-Asian Aerosols on Arctic and Regional Climate

Description: A collaborative Indo-Norwegian research project aimed at quantifying the regional and Arctic climate impacts of South Asian aerosol emissions through improved emission inventories and climate analysis.

Position: Junior Research Fellow

Host Institution: Utkal University, India

Partner Institution: CICERO Centre for International Climate Research, Norway

Contribution: Developed spatially resolved emission inventories and analysed observational datasets to support investigations of South Asian aerosol emissions and their regional and Arctic climate implications. Contributed to scientific data visualisations and technical project reports.
