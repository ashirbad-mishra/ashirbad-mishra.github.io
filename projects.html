Projects

Research programmes

Selected research programmes spanning atmospheric pollution, greenhouse gases, emission inventories, Earth observation and environmental health.

Greenhouse gases · Emission inventories

High-Resolution Methane Emission Inventory for India

Development of a 0.1° × 0.1° gridded methane emission dataset for India, integrating source-specific activity data, emission factors and spatial allocation to characterize sectoral contributions and emission hotspots.

Air pollution · Atmospheric composition

High-Resolution Air Pollution Emission Inventories

Development and spatial analysis of high-resolution inventories for particulate matter and multiple air pollutants across India to identify hotspots and critical source sectors.

Air pollution · Environmental health

Source-Specific Air Pollution and Public Health

Investigation of source-specific particulate emissions and their association with public-health outcomes, linking emission information with disease-prevalence data.

Transport emissions · Climate action

Transport and Non-Exhaust Emissions

Research on transport-related emissions and mitigation, including policy-oriented analysis and assessment of non-exhaust particulate sources.
