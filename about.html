Academic profile

I am a Doctoral Fellow in the Department of Environmental Science at Berhampur University, India. My doctoral research focuses on the development of high-resolution particulate and indirect greenhouse-gas emission inventories and their teleconnection with disease prevalence across India.

I have half a decade experience in integrating emission inventories, atmospheric observations, Earth observation and geospatial analysis to understand complex environmental problems across spatial and temporal scales. I work at the interface of emissions, atmospheric processes and environmental health, using spatially resolved datasets and interdisciplinary approaches to understand where pollutants originate, how they evolve and disperse in the atmosphere, and how these changes influence air quality, climate and human exposure.

My goal is to translate this understanding into spatially explicit scientific evidence that can support effective air-quality and climate mitigation. Going forward, I aim to develop an integrated research framework combining high-resolution emission inventories, Earth observation, atmospheric modelling and environmental-health analysis to investigate the interactions between emissions, air pollution, climate and health impact from regional to national scales.
