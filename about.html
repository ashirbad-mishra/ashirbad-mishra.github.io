About

An atmospheric researcher focused on measurable sources and evidence.

I am a Doctoral Fellow in the Department of Environmental Science at Berhampur University, India. My doctoral research focuses on high-resolution emission inventories of particulate pollutants and greenhouse gases across India and their teleconnection with disease prevalence.

Profile

My research experience spans emission inventory development, geospatial analysis, satellite data processing, observational datasets, scientific visualisation and atmospheric-source analysis. I am interested in building an integrated pathway from bottom-up emission estimates to atmospheric observations and modelling.

My longer-term direction is to work at the intersection of atmospheric composition, Earth observation, greenhouse-gas monitoring, emission modelling and evidence-based mitigation.
