Academic profile

I am a Doctoral Fellow in the Department of Environmental Science at Berhampur University, India. My doctoral research focuses on the development of high-resolution particulate and indirect greenhouse-gas emission inventories and their teleconnection with disease prevalence across India.

My research experience includes comprehensive high-resolution gridded emission inventories for greenhouse gases and particulate matter, pollution hotspot identification, observational-data analysis, satellite data processing, spatial analysis and environmental-health applications.

Research interests include greenhouse-gas emission inventory, urban air quality and source attribution, air-pollution exposure and environmental health, satellite remote sensing, atmospheric modelling, and emission mitigation strategies and policy implications.
